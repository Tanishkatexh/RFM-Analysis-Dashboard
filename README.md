## 📌 Executive Summary

This Power BI dashboard leverages **RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) Analysis** to segment customers and drive retention-focused marketing strategies. ## 🚨 Problem Statement

### The Growth Paradox

Despite year-over-year sales growth of **24.9%**, three critical customer health indicators are declining:

1. **Declining Recency (-0.7% YoY):** Customers are purchasing less frequently in recent months
2. **Declining Frequency (-0.7% YoY):** Average purchase count per customer is decreasing
3. **Declining Monetary Value (-0.8% YoY):** Average customer spending is trending downward

### Root Cause Analysis

**Hypothesis:** Revenue growth is driven by seasonal holiday spikes (November $2M+ peak) rather than sustainable customer acquisition or retention. Without intervention:

- **Q1 2024 Risk:** Revenue cliff after holiday season (historical Jan-Oct flatness)
- **Customer Churn Risk:** Studies show 26 customers leave silently for every 1 who complains
- **Retention Economics:** Retaining customers is 5-25x cheaper than acquiring new ones, yet retained customers spend 31-67% more than new customers

### Business Impact If Metrics Continue Declining

- **Projected Q1 Sales:** ~$1.2M - $1.4M (vs. $1.97M current)
- **Revenue Loss Estimate:** -35-40% post-holiday
- **Customer Health:** At-Risk and Lost segments growing; Champions potentially churning

## 💡 Business Insights

### Insight #1: Seasonal Dependency Risk ⚠️

**Data:** November peak at $2M vs. Jan-Oct average of $1.3M

**Interpretation:** Your business is 60% dependent on holiday season sales. This is unsustainable and masks underlying customer retention problems.

**Impact:** Without retention strategies in off-season months, you face significant revenue volatility.

---

### Insight #2: Declining Customer Engagement (RFM Deterioration) 📉

**Data:** Recency -0.7%, Frequency -0.7%, Monetary -0.8% YoY

**Interpretation:** Declining engagement frequency is a classic early warning sign. Customers who purchased regularly but now visit occasionally indicate potential churn.

**Business Risk:**
- Customers are buying less often
- Customers are spending less per transaction
- Customers aren't returning as quickly after last purchase
- Churn risk is increasing silently

---

### Insight #3: Untapped Cross-Sell Opportunity 🎯

**Data:** Three distinct categories (Technology, Furniture, Office Supplies) with minimal bundling

**Interpretation:** Customers are buying single categories, not bundled solutions. A customer buying Technology is not being offered complementary Furniture or Office Supplies.

**Opportunity:** Bundle offers increase customer satisfaction and average order value by 25-40%.

---

### Insight #4: At-Risk Segment Growth 🔴

**Data:** Segment split shows growing At-Risk and Lost customer populations

**Critical Finding:** If At-Risk segment is growing while Frequency and Monetary decline, you're experiencing silent churn. Reducing churn by even 1-2% leads to significant revenue gains.

## 🛠️ Solution Board & Recommended Strategies

### SOLUTION #1: Implement Seasonal Bundling Strategy

**Problem:** November spike not replicated Jan-Oct; single-category purchases

**Implementation:**

```
BUNDLE CONCEPT: "Complete Office Solutions"

├─ Tech Bundle: Desk Setup
│  ├─ Includes: Monitor/Laptop + Desk + Desk Organizer
│  ├─ Price: 12-15% discount vs. individual
│  └─ Target: Emerging & At-Risk segments
│
├─ Home Office Bundle: Remote Work Essentials
│  ├─ Includes: Chair + Lighting + Organization
│  ├─ Price: 10% discount
│  └─ Target: Loyal Customers & Champions
│
└─ Starter Bundle: New Customer Offer
   ├─ Includes: Best-seller from each category
   ├─ Price: 20% discount
   └─ Target: Lost Customers & Emerging
```

**Expected Impact:**
- Increase Average Order Value by 25-30%
- Reduce seasonality dips by 15-20%
- Drive recency recovery through bundled promotions

### SOLUTION #2: Targeted Retention Campaign by Segment

**Problem:** Recency, Frequency, Monetary all declining despite revenue growth

#### At-Risk Customers (60+ days since purchase)
```
Campaign: "We Miss You" Re-engagement
├─ Action: Personalized email from CEO/Leadership
├─ Offer: 20% off + free shipping
├─ Follow-up: SMS reminder at day 75
└─ Success Metric: Return to purchase within 30 days
```

#### Declining Monetary Value Customers
```
Campaign: "Upgrade Your Order"
├─ Action: 1:1 recommendation based on purchase history
├─ Offer: Upsell to premium tier + 15% discount
├─ Cross-sell: Complementary category bundle
└─ Success Metric: Increase AOV by 25%
```

#### At-Risk Champions (High value + low recency)
```
Campaign: "VIP Exclusive Access"
├─ Action: Dedicated account manager outreach
├─ Offer: Early access to new products
├─ Incentive: 2x loyalty points on next purchase
└─ Success Metric: Restore purchase frequency
```

**Expected Impact:**
- Reduce churn by 15-20%
- Restore recency to <150 days average
- Increase frequency back to 6+ purchases/year

### SOLUTION #5: Predictive Churn Prevention Using RFM Scoring

**Problem:** Silent churn in At-Risk segment; declining metrics hide severity

**RFM Scoring Model (1-5 scale per metric):**

```
Score 5-5-5 (Champions)
├─ Action: VIP loyalty program enrollment
├─ Frequency: Quarterly touch-point
└─ Focus: Retention & upsell

Score 4-4-4 (Loyal Customers)
├─ Action: Monthly personalized offers
├─ Frequency: Bi-weekly emails
└─ Focus: Retention & cross-sell

Score 3-3-3 (Emerging)
├─ Action: Weekly engagement campaigns
├─ Frequency: Regular promotion offers
└─ Focus: Upsell to next tier

Score 2-2-2 (At-Risk) ⚠️
├─ Action: Immediate intervention (60+ days)
├─ Frequency: Multi-channel (email, SMS, phone)
└─ Focus: Win-back offer + product recommendation

Score 1-1-1 (Lost) 🔴
├─ Action: Last-chance campaign
├─ Frequency: Limited time (30-day window)
└─ Focus: One-time incentive to return
```

**Expected Impact:**
- Reduce silent churn by 25-35%
- Identify at-risk customers before they leave
- Automate retention actions based on RFM thresholds
